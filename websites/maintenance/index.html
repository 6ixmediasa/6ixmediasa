What actually breaks

Sites rarely fail dramatically. A plugin updates and a form stops sending. An SSL certificate lapses and browsers start warning visitors away. Someone changes a price in one place and forgets the other three. Maintenance is mostly about catching these before a customer does.

  • Weekly off-site backups you can restore from
  • Core, plugin and theme updates, tested
  • Uptime monitoring with alerts
  • Security scanning and malware cleanup

Content changes included

Every plan includes a monthly allowance of small changes — new prices, a staff photo, an updated trading hours notice. Send them on WhatsApp and they get done, without a quote for every line.

Reporting you can read

A short monthly note covering what was updated, what traffic looked like, and anything worth acting on. No forty-page automated PDF that nobody opens.

Common questions

Do I have to take a care plan?
No. It is optional and you can cancel any time. If you prefer, we handle changes as they come up and bill for the time.
Can you maintain a site you did not build?
Usually yes. Send us the link and login and we will audit it first, then tell you honestly whether it is worth maintaining or whether rebuilding is cheaper in the long run.
